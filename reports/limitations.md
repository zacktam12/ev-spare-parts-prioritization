## Source Limitations

- **NHTSA data is US-only**: Complaint and recall data reflects US driving conditions and vehicle usage, not Ethiopian conditions.
- **Marketplace listings ≠ sales**: Counts on Adey Market represent listings, not confirmed vehicle registrations or sales volumes.
- **Exchange rates**: Any USD conversions use the rate observed on the collection date and may not reflect import costs.
- **Part compatibility**: Catalogue listings may not guarantee exact fitment without OEM part number confirmation.
